How to use this site

Follow these steps to build an application for your trade or profession.

  1. Step 1

    Create your account

    Choose Start Free to register, or Login if you already have an account. Your dashboard keeps your saved CVs and cover letters together.

  2. Step 2

    Add your CV

    Select Create CV and enter your details, skills, experience and education. Or upload a text-based PDF or Word (.docx) file and select Import & Fill CV Fields. Check the imported details, then click Save CV.

  3. Step 3

    Match a job

    Select a CV from your dashboard and click Job Match. Paste or upload the job description, then choose Analyse Job Against This CV to see matching skills, gaps and suggestions.

  4. Step 4

    Tailor, review and edit

    Click Tailor My CV to create a separate version for the job. Review the highlighted changes and edit the New CV fields. Choose Use original text to reject a section?s changes, then click Save reviewed CV. Your original CV stays unchanged.

  5. Step 5

    Generate a cover letter

    After analysing the job description, return to the dashboard, select the CV you want to use and click Cover Letter, then Generate Cover Letter. It uses your latest analysed job description and is saved under My Cover Letters.

  6. Step 6

    Download and apply

    On the dashboard, select your CV and click Choose PDF Template. Choose Modern, Classic or Executive to download a PDF. Open a saved cover letter to download its PDF too. Always check your documents before sending them.
