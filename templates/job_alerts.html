How matching works

We search Adzuna's UK vacancies, not every job on the internet. Search terms and location are sent to Adzuna; your full CV and contact details are not.

We rank adverts using words shared with your selected CV. Matching is a guide, not a qualification check or a guarantee of suitability. Read each full advert before applying.

Remote and hybrid filters require those arrangements to be mentioned in the advert. A minimum salary filters out adverts without a suitable stated salary, which may reduce results.

Only new matches are emailed. If there are no new matches, no email is sent for that daily or weekly check. You can change your frequency, update your preferences or unsubscribe at any time.
